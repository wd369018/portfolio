SETUP NOTES — 2 quick things to finish before you go live
============================================================

1) MAKE THE CONTACT FORM ACTUALLY SEND EMAILS
   Go to https://web3forms.com, get a FREE access key with your email,
   then open main.js and replace:
       const WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY = "YOUR_ACCESS_KEY_HERE";
   with your real key. The form will then email you directly,
   including any CV / cover letter attachments visitors send.

2) ADD YOUR REAL RESUME & COVER LETTER FILES
   The download buttons point to resume.pdf and cover-letter.pdf.
   Drop your actual PDFs into the same folder as index.html with
   those exact names.
